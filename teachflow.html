01 / THE PROBLEM

Voice assistants can understand you.But can they operate the apps you use?

USER
"Order a pizza from Domino's."
VOICE ASSISTANT

✓ Understood

✕ Can't reliably operate third-party app UI

Understanding language is solved. Acting inside apps that expose no API is not.

      06 / RECOVER · ADAPTIVE RECOVERY

      The screen changed.The goal didn't.

      Every action is observed and verified before the next. When the target isn't found, TeachFlow recovers and re-observes. It asks you only when it genuinely can't proceed.

      Dismiss safe interruptions

      Promos, rating prompts and tooltips are classified as non-blocking and closed.

      Re-ground by meaning

      If a button moved or was renamed slightly, the step is re-matched by role and text, not position.

      Ask when genuinely stuck

      Unknown screens, logins and missing data stop the run and hand the decision to you.

        KNOWN LIMITATIONS

        What it can't do yet.

        • Accessibility metadata varies across applications.
        • Custom-rendered controls may expose limited semantics.
        • Major UI redesigns can require clarification.
        • Cross-app generalization depends on semantic compatibility.
        • Authentication and payment remain human-controlled.

        We prefer a safe handoff over an unsafe action.
